# Raise Investment Domain

This glossary captures the public language of Raise Investment so interview discussion stays precise. It describes the product concepts that Raise uses publicly and does not infer legal or implementation structure.

## People and commercial relationship

**Member**:
A customer who pays for ongoing access to a Raise Investment position and is entitled to its gains under the membership terms.
_Avoid_: Depositor, borrower, shareholder, account holder when the specific account is unclear

**Membership**:
The continuing commercial relationship that gives a Member access to a Raise-funded market position in exchange for a monthly fee.
_Avoid_: Savings plan, loan, brokerage subscription

**Membership Fee**:
The recurring amount paid by a Member as Raise revenue. Raise states that this payment is not invested.
_Avoid_: Contribution, deposit, premium, invested payment

**Institutional Capital Partner**:
An organization that supplies the capital used for the initial market exposure and recovers that capital when the membership exits or another disclosed liquidation occurs.
_Avoid_: Lender to the Member, depositor

## Position and value

**Advanced Principal**:
The capital supplied through Raise's institutional partners and placed into market exposure on a Member's behalf. Raise states that the Member does not own this principal and does not owe it as personal debt.
_Avoid_: Member deposit, Member contribution, loan balance

**Position**:
The total market exposure associated with a Membership, including the Advanced Principal and any Gains.
_Avoid_: Savings balance, Member-owned principal

**Market Value**:
Raise's term for the current total value of the Position, including Advanced Principal and Gains.
_Avoid_: Withdrawable balance, Take-Home Value

**Gain**:
The portion of Position value above the Advanced Principal that Raise says belongs to the Member. A decline can reduce this amount to zero.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed return, dividend, interest

**Take-Home Value**:
Raise's term for the amount attributed to the Member, described publicly as Gains above the Advanced Principal and paid after an Exit.
_Avoid_: Market Value, account balance, guaranteed payout

## Market exposure

**Buffered ETF**:
A defined-outcome exchange-traded fund linked to the S&P 500 that exchanges some upside potential for a targeted downside buffer during a stated Outcome Window.
_Avoid_: Insurance policy, guaranteed principal protection, uncapped index fund

**Outcome Window**:
The fixed period, described by Raise as 12 months, during which a Buffered ETF's cap and buffer apply. The stated outcome depends on holding for the full window.
_Avoid_: Lock-up period, guarantee period

**Buffer**:
The targeted portion of market decline that a Buffered ETF is designed to absorb during its Outcome Window. It is not a general guarantee against every loss or exit timing.
_Avoid_: Full loss protection, insured value

**Cap**:
The maximum upside participation of the Buffered ETF during a particular Outcome Window.
_Avoid_: Fee ceiling, contribution limit

**Uncapped S&P 500 Exposure**:
The portion of a Position above the protected principal sleeve that participates in S&P 500 market movement without the Buffered ETF's upside cap.
_Avoid_: Risk-free gains, protected value

## Lifecycle

**Investment Account**:
Raise's customer-facing account for the Investment product. Public material does not establish whether this term is identical to any underlying brokerage or custodial account.
_Avoid_: Brokerage account unless Raise confirms the legal and provider structure

**Onboarding**:
The pre-approval process in which a prospective Member provides account level and demographic information, schedules a call, discusses financial goals, and confirms understanding of the product.
_Avoid_: Automatic signup, instant account opening

**Exit**:
Cancellation of a Membership followed by sale of the Position, recovery of Advanced Principal for capital partners, and transfer of the Take-Home Value to the Member's linked bank account.
_Avoid_: Partial withdrawal, redemption of principal

**Withdrawal**:
A term Raise currently limits to a full Exit. Public material says partial withdrawal is not currently available.
_Avoid_: Routine transfer, partial cash-out
